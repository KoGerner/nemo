# Glossary — the abbreviations in these files

What each abbreviation in Marschkamp's files stands for, in plain words. The first time one of them
appears in something nemo shows, its plain words follow it in brackets. The words of business
continuity itself (BIA, MTPD, RTO and the rest) are nemo's and are not repeated here.

- **AR**: part of the product name of the ticket system from BMC
- **BCMS**: the business continuity management system, the way the company runs business continuity
- **BMC**: the maker of the Remedy ticket system
- **CEO**: the chief executive officer, head of the management board
- **CFO**: the chief financial officer
- **COO**: the chief operating officer, here the board member for production
- **CSB**: the business software used at the Marschkamp site, named after its maker
- **DE**: Germany
- **EDI**: electronic data interchange, orders and invoices exchanged directly between two companies' systems
- **ERP**: the business software for orders, stock and invoices
- **FTE**: full-time equivalents, staff counted as full-time posts
- **GDPR**: the European data protection regulation
- **HACCP**: hazard analysis and critical control points, the food safety system that controls hazards in production
- **HR**: human resources, the personnel department
- **HRA**: the commercial register for partnerships
- **HRB**: the commercial register for companies
- **IAM**: identity and access management, who may log in to what
- **ID**: an identifier
- **IEC**: the International Electrotechnical Commission, which publishes technical standards, among them one on risk assessment methods
- **ISO**: the International Organization for Standardization, which publishes international standards, among them one on risk management
- **IT**: the computers, networks and software
- **JIRA**: the ticket and planning software from Atlassian, as some records write it
- **KG**: a German limited partnership
- **MAWI**: the name of the business software used at the Breitenburg site
- **MS**: Microsoft, as the records shorten it
- **PI**: the register court at Pinneberg
- **QA**: quality assurance
- **QM**: quality management
- **SAP**: the maker of the finance software
- **VAT**: value added tax
