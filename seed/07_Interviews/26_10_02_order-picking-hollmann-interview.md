# Order picking: 2026 re-interview

> Written for the demo pack, not in any prior record. This year's interview, for comparison with prior BIA record 1.3.

**Date:** 2 October 2026, 10:30
**Participants:** Sven Hollmann (Head of Order Picking); Dirk Wohlleben (Maintenance Manager); Franziska Dohm (BCM Officer, interviewer). Henning Barsch (Plant Manager, process owner) sent apologies.
**Record:** re-interview of order picking for the 2026 cycle. Last year's record is prior BIA record 1.3. Recorded at the Marschkamp plant.

---

Franziska Dohm: Sven, last year's record is from September 2024. What has changed in order picking?

Sven Hollmann: Three things. We pick for Westerkoog Retail now, on top of our own branches. We run a second shift since spring. And the weighing is done on handheld scanners, not on the old scales.

Franziska Dohm: The impact first. Harm to people?

Sven Hollmann: No. A standstill harms nobody.

Franziska Dohm: Financially? Last year: none, the goods can be picked later.

Sven Hollmann: For our branches, still true. Westerkoog's orders have a delivery window. If they're not picked, they're not loaded, and the penalty is theirs to claim from day three. So medium at 72 hours, high after a week.

Franziska Dohm: Legal and image?

Sven Hollmann: Legal follows the contract: medium at 72 hours, high at 168. Image I'd leave at none. Inside the group nobody sees us.

Franziska Dohm: Operationally?

Sven Hollmann: As before: low at 72 hours, high after a week.

Franziska Dohm: RTO and MTPD? Last year 72 hours and more than 168.

Sven Hollmann: I'd keep both. That's Henning's call, though.

Franziska Dohm: The second shift. How many people now?

Sven Hollmann: The pool is 36 in normal operation, it was 30. In emergency we need 18, it was 15.

Franziska Dohm: Who leads the second shift?

Sven Hollmann: Nobody, officially. The position is approved but not filled. Until then I cover it myself, or the early-shift lead stays on.

Franziska Dohm: The scanners. What do they need?

Sven Hollmann: The merchandise management system for the order data, and the network. If they fail, we can go back to the old scales, but only at about half the speed.

Franziska Dohm: Technicians, Dirk?

Dirk Wohlleben: Unchanged for picking: eight in the pool, four in emergency.

Franziska Dohm: To sum up: picking for Westerkoog adds financial and legal impact, medium at 72 hours and high at 168. The picking pool goes from 30 to 36, and emergency from 15 to 18. The second-shift lead is approved but not filled. The scanners can fall back to the old scales at about half speed. RTO and MTPD unchanged for now, for Henning to confirm. Thank you.
