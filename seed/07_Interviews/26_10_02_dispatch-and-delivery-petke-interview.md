# Goods dispatch and delivery: 2026 re-interview

> Written for the demo pack, not in any prior record. This year's interview, for comparison with prior BIA record 1.4.

**Date:** 2 October 2026, 13:00
**Participants:** Diana Petke (Head of Fleet and Transport); Anke Reimers (Logistics Coordinator); Henning Barsch (Plant Manager, process owner); Franziska Dohm (BCM Officer, interviewer)
**Record:** re-interview of goods dispatch and delivery for the 2026 cycle. Last year's record is prior BIA record 1.4. Recorded at the Marschkamp plant.

---

Franziska Dohm: Last year's record is from September 2024. Diana, what has changed?

Diana Petke: We deliver to Westerkoog Retail's central warehouse now, in fixed time slots. We plan the tours in a route planner since the summer. And the external forwarder is under contract.

Franziska Dohm: The impact. Harm to people?

Diana Petke: No.

Franziska Dohm: Financially? Last year: none.

Henning Barsch: Not any more. A missed Westerkoog slot counts as a short delivery. From day three the penalty runs. Medium at 72 hours, high after a week.

Franziska Dohm: Legal?

Henning Barsch: The same contract: medium at 72 hours, high at 168.

Franziska Dohm: Image?

Diana Petke: Last year none, because we could switch to an external fleet. That's still true. But a new customer notices every missed slot. Low at 72 hours.

Franziska Dohm: Operationally?

Diana Petke: As before. Up to 72 hours we can deliver later, then low, and after a week high.

Franziska Dohm: RTO and MTPD? Last year 72 hours and more than 168.

Henning Barsch: Keep them.

Franziska Dohm: The route planner. What is it, and who owns it?

Anke Reimers: It plans the tours and the slots, and it sends Westerkoog the delivery notice. It came in with the contract. Who owns it? Purchasing bought it, IT runs the server, and we use it. Nobody has been named.

Franziska Dohm: If it fails?

Anke Reimers: We plan by hand from the order lists, as before. For our branches that works. For Westerkoog's slots it's slow, and the delivery notice has to go by e-mail.

Franziska Dohm: And the forwarder?

Diana Petke: Schlugge Lagerlogistik. Last year it was "possible". Now we have a framework agreement: up to five lorries on a day's notice.

Franziska Dohm: Fleet staff and supervisors?

Diana Petke: Unchanged: 15 in normal operation, 10 in emergency.

Franziska Dohm: To sum up: deliveries to Westerkoog add financial and legal impact, medium at 72 hours and high at 168, and image low at 72. Tours are planned in a route planner that has no named owner, with a manual fallback that is slow for Westerkoog. Schlugge Lagerlogistik is now contracted for up to five lorries on a day's notice. RTO and MTPD unchanged. Thank you.
