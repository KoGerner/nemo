Franziska Dohm: Thank you for the time, Henrik. First the process itself: what does it do, and where does it sit?

Henrik Stoltenberg: The IT financials process covers managing and optimising IT's financial resources, to ensure the financial stability and efficiency of the IT department. It includes monitoring and analysing IT spending, budgeting and financial planning, and assessing the financial impact of IT projects. The process ensures that all financial transactions and decisions comply with legal and internal policies, and assesses the impact on areas such as finance, law, image and operations. It is a support process, and its process owner is the head of IT financial management. The organisational unit is IT and organisation.
