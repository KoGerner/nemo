# Operational risk register — Marschkamp

Written for the demo pack under ADR-0034: no prior BIA record contains this register. Each row turns a cause the
records state (§5 of the named record) into a risk, and lists the emergency operation the same record states (§6)
as its control. Severity is the record's own potential-threat rating; likelihood is a judgement. Every row says so.

**Status:** draft, not endorsed. It carries no approval signature because no one has approved it.
**Basis:** ISO 31000 / IEC 31010, 5×5 matrix, on the BIA method's own severity scale.

## Scoring legend

- **S (severity)**: the potential-threat rating of the worst-hit process, taken from its prior BIA record, on the
  method's scale: 1 none · 2 low · 3 medium · 4 high · 5 very high. Not a judgement.
- **L (likelihood)**: a judgement written for the demo pack: 1 rare · 2 unlikely · 3 possible · 4 likely · 5 almost
  certain.
- **Inh.** = L × S. **Bands:** 1–4 low · 5–9 medium · 10–15 high · 16–25 critical.
- **Residual by rule:** rS is one lower where the record's emergency operation keeps the process running in reduced
  form. rL is one lower where a control lowers the chance of this risk occurring: a four-eyes check where the risk is
  misuse or error, or an approved alternative or a channel agreed beforehand; a control that only recovers after the
  event leaves rL = L.
- **"No control stated in any record"** is a finding: the row stays open until a control is defined.
